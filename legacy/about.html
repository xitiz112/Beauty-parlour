Opened so the chair could slow down.

Anisha Basnet started Liora in Jhamsikhel after years of working rooms that booked too tightly. The studio keeps a short day on purpose: color, bridal, skin, and nails share one quiet floor, and every tool is sanitized between guests.

We take time with color so it still looks like you on a Tuesday. If a look will not grow out kindly, we say so before the first foil goes in.
